## Why I Built This
Taking part in the Stardance Challenge was a really big reminder of how relevant personal websites still are when getting into the tech sector! Resumes are cool, but having a space on the web where you control every single pixel and layout yourself makes such a huge difference. :)

It also acts as my own personal gallery! I wanted a spot to host my game builds, 3D stuff, and software projects so I can look back at them after a loooooong time and feel that nostalgia. :D

---

## What's Inside
The site opens with a dark theme hero banner with floating bubbles and custom typography. As you scroll down, there are live previews of my game builds and software projects, a quick look at the everyday tools I use like Xcode, VS Code, Figma, Photoshop, Premiere, and Canva, plus a quick section about my goals in robotics engineering and highlights like the Future Innovators Award.
